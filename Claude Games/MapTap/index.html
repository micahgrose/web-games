How it works

Five places per round. You are told the place and given a piece of its history. Spin the globe, tap where you think it is, then lock it in.

Scoring is by distance. Within 25 km is a perfect 100. It falls away from there — about 85 at 250 km, 50 at 1,000 km, single digits past 3,000 km.

The right country is worth 10 points on top, so a near miss on the correct side of a border beats a near miss on the wrong side. It can never push you past 100.

The last questions count more. Multipliers run 1, 1, 2, 3, 3 — a flawless round is exactly 1000.

No borders, no labels. Land, ocean, and what you know.
