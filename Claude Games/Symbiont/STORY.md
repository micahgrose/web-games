## The premise
You are a **symbiote** — a torn scrap of a hive-mind, flung across the dark when
your world was used up. You fall to a quiet, warm, living planet. **Alone you are a
dying blob**: leaking, slow, unmaking in the open air. You survive the only way your
kind does — by **sinking into a living body and wearing it.**

Your essence is **poison.** Every host rots and crumbles; flesh was never built to
hold you. So you keep moving, keep taking new bodies. **To live is to spread, and
you have never, on any world, failed to spread.**

The planet *notices.* Animals bolt. People whisper of a sickness wearing familiar
faces. The brave ones come — torches, then blades, then a **champion** who will not
yield. You take them too. Then the world is quiet, and you wait for the next one.

**Tone:** calm, surgical, predatory. It never panics, never mourns, never doubts.
The world is innocent and warm; you are the patient wrongness moving through it. The
dread is that nothing reaches it.

---

## The name
It does **not name itself** — naming is a thing small, frightened, mortal creatures
do. To it, "I" is enough. The *people* give it a name out of fear (Act 3). Draft
human word: **"the Worn"** (because it wears them). Flag if you want a different word.

## The arc (each act introduces ONE mechanic, justified by story)
| Act | Place | New body / mechanic | Beat (scale escalates, voice stays cold) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Arrival** | dusk meadow, crash site | possession, move/jump, **decay** | you fall, dying; take a critter; learn the body sours; reach the treeline |
| **2 — Spread** | farms & fields | a **fast** host → dash | cross open land; first humans see you and run; fear is just noise |
| **3 — Alarm** | the village | a **heavy** host → pound/break | smash a barricade; they've named you; a torch-hunter comes and fails |
| **4 — The Heroes Rise** | walls & rooftops | a **flying** host | soldiers, order, verticality; walls mean nothing; you go up |
| **5 — The Champion** | the reckoning | **elite** that resists possession until wounded | the one who won't break — wound it, take it anyway, the world goes still |

## FULL SCRIPT (draft) — inner voice, each line fires once at a trigger zone

### ACT 1 — "Arrival"
1. *(wake, dying, exposed)* — `Cold. The air is thin and bright and wrong. I am leaking into it.`
2. *(a critter wanders near)* — `Warm. Small. A heartbeat under fur. I will wear it.`
3. *(first possession)* — `...there. Legs. Lungs. Breath that was never mine. Mine now.`
4. *(open ground — move)* — `It knows how to run. Let it run.`
5. *(a ledge — jump)* — `Up. The legs want to leap. Permit them.`
6. *(body starts to sour — decay)* — `It curdles already. They never last. They were not built to hold me.`
7. *(body crumbles — exposed again)* — `Empty. The open air unmakes me. Another, then. There is always another.`
8. *(act end — farm lights through trees)* — `Lights in the dark. Warm bodies, kept close together. Convenient.`

### ACT 2 — "Spread"
1. *(enter the fields)* — `More of them here. Penned. Fed. Fattened for me without knowing it.`
2. *(meet the fast host)* — `This one is quick. Quickness is useful. Quickness is mine.`
3. *(learn dash)* — `Faster. The ground barely touches us now.`
4. *(first human sees you, flees)* — `It runs upright. It makes a sound at the others. Warning them. As if warning helps.`
5. *(act end)* — `They scatter toward their walls. I follow at my leisure. They tire. I do not.`

### ACT 3 — "Alarm"
1. *(enter village)* — `They have shut their doors. They have made a wall of their things. Quaint.`
2. *(meet the heavy host)* — `Big. Slow. Strong. I have no use for fear, but I have use for strong.`
3. *(learn pound / break)* — `Their wall is a suggestion. I am not in the habit of taking suggestions.`
4. *(they've named you)* — `They have given me a name now — "the Worn." As if a name is a leash. As if it changes what comes.`
5. *(torch-hunter appears)* — `One comes with fire. Brave. Fire does not frighten what has no skin to keep.`
6. *(hunter falls / act end)* — `It was warm, too, for a while.`

### ACT 4 — "The Heroes Rise"
1. *(walls & soldiers)* — `Now they bring order. Lines. Discipline. Order is only fear that has stopped trembling.`
2. *(meet the flying host)* — `This one has wings. I have wanted to go up.`
3. *(learn flight)* — `Their walls were built to keep things out at ground level. Small imaginations.`
4. *(over the city)* — `From up here they are not people. They are a supply.`
5. *(act end — the champion is named)* — `They speak of one who will end me. They believe it. Belief is also just noise.`

### ACT 5 — "The Champion"
1. *(the champion stands)* — `One body that does not run. It has decided to be the wall. Very well.`
2. *(it resists possession)* — `I reach in — and it holds. It knows my shape. Someone has felt me before and lived to teach it. ...Interesting. Not enough.`
3. *(wound it)* — `Everything opens, given enough pressure. Even resolve. Especially resolve.`
4. *(take it)* — `There. Even this. Even the best of them.`
5. *(world goes still — ending)* — `Quiet now. The warmth goes out of the world, room by room. ...There are other worlds. There are always other worlds.`
